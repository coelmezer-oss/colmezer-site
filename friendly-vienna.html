Friendly Vienna

Friendly Vienna:
A UX Exploration Series.

A small contribution to the city I love.

As a designer living in Vienna, I wanted to find a way to be useful to the people I share this city with.

We use the same apps and websites every day. Sometimes they work great, but other times they could use a little extra care. Friendly Vienna is my personal project exploring how we can make these daily digital interactions more intuitive and accessible for everyone.

It's not about changing everything. It's just about making things a little friendlier, one step at a time.
